01 / A note from Daisy

Some things are best said with flowers.

I started Daisy’s Flower Shop because flowers say what words sometimes can’t: I love you. I’m sorry. Congratulations. Thank you.

Growing up around gardens, I fell in love with color, texture and natural beauty. What began as arrangements for friends, family and local events became a business rooted in listening and care. A simple gift or a 200-person wedding deserves the same heart.

With love, Daisy

08 / Common Questions

Before we talk flowers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is available by arrangement, with fees based on distance. Email your address so we can check.

How far ahead should I order?

Allow 24–48 hours for everyday flowers. For weddings, we recommend getting in touch 3–6 months in advance.

Can I choose my colors?

Absolutely. Share your colors, style, occasion and budget, and we’ll design something personal with the best seasonal flowers available.

Do you offer workshops?

We offer seasonal workshops for individuals, teams and private groups. Get in touch to ask about upcoming dates.
